Common Questions

Everything parents want to know

What ages do you teach? +

Our Fine Arts program is for children ages 4–15 across three tiers: Little Creative Explorers (4–7), Creative Artists (7–10), and Young Masters (10–15). Language classes are available for ages 6+. The Creative Wellness program is open to kids, teens, and adults.

What is the class schedule? +

Weekend classes run every Saturday from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM. You can join on any Saturday — no need to wait for a new batch. We also offer compensation for missed classes.

How many classes are in each program? +

Each Fine Arts tier includes 10 classes (8 regular + 2 bonus). The Language & Heritage Program is a 16-class structured program (12 + 4 classes). Creative Wellness programs also include 10 classes (8 + 2).

What is the Creative Wellness Program? +

It's our newest program using art as a tool for emotional wellbeing. Kids & Teens explore Neuro Art, Mindful Creativity, and Emotional Expression. Adults enjoy Stress Relief Art, Mandala & Zentangle, Creative Journaling, and more.

How do ISD credits work? +

We prepare students for Credit-By-Exam (CBE) tests. Passing allows students to earn World Language or Fine Arts credits for high school — a huge advantage for college applications. We provide portfolio guidance and credit pathway support.

Where are you located? +

We are located at Stacy Montessori, Stacy Road, McKinney, TX 75070. Call us at 469-955-2323 for directions or more information.
